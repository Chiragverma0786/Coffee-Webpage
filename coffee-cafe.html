The Philosophy

Coffee engineered with uncompromising patience.

We founded Coffee Café with a single guiding thesis: haste is the enemy of nuance. Every bean we roast is sourced directly from high-altitude estates above 1,800 meters, roasted in micro-batches twice weekly, and rested for exactly 48 hours to unlock complex aromatic esters.

Our baristas operate not as speed servers, but as precision sensory extractors — calibrating TDS (Total Dissolved Solids), water mineral hardness, and grind particle distribution with scientific rigor.

Pour-Over Protocol

The 4-Step V60 Extraction Guide.

Follow our barista champion recipe. Press start to run our live synchronized brew timer with pulse audio chimes.

01

The Bloom & Degassing

Pour 50g water in concentric circles. Let freshly roasted coffee degas CO2 completely.

Duration: 45s  ·  Target: 50g
02

First Spiral Infusion

Pour steadily from center outwards to reach 150g weight. Maintain steady slurry agitation.

Duration: 45s  ·  Target: 150g
03

Body Extraction

Gentle center pour bringing total weight to 250g, drawing out caramelized honey notes.

Duration: 45s  ·  Target: 250g
04

Final Drawdown & Swirl

Top off to 300g. Give one gentle carafe swirl for an immaculate flat spent coffee bed.

Duration: 45s  ·  Target: 300g
