Our Story

Coffee, made the
unhurried way.

We started Coffee Café with one rule: nothing rushed. Beans are roasted in small batches, cupped every morning, and poured one cup at a time — because coffee made in a hurry tastes like it.

Every bag on our shelf is traceable to the farm it came from, and every barista behind our counter has spent months learning to read a pour the way you'd read a room.

— The Roastery Team

From Farm to Cup

Our Process

Three unhurried steps between a coffee cherry and the cup in your hands.

01

Sourced

Direct-trade relationships with growers across Ethiopia, Colombia and Sumatra — paid fairly, every harvest.

02

Roasted

Small-batch roasted in-house, twice a week, and rested for 48 hours before it ever reaches a grinder.

03

Brewed

Hand-poured to order, dialed in cup by cup — never sitting on a burner, never rushed out the window.
